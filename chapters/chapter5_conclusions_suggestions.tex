\chapter{Conclusions and Suggestions}

The observed differences in salt stress tolerance among mint species are consistent with previous reports indicating species-specific physiological responses to salinity \textcite{Singh2022}. The mitigation of salt stress effects by biochar observed in this study aligns with earlier findings on the role of biochar in improving substrate conditions under saline environments \textcite{Joseph2021, Haider2022}.

Overall, the findings of this study support previous research highlighting the potential of biochar as a sustainable peat alternative, particularly under abiotic stress conditions such as salinity \textcite{Dumroese2018, Joseph2021}.
